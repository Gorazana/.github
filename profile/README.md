## Native first. Explicit ownership.

Reuse native Goravel infrastructure and compose focused capabilities. Choose managed packages, publishable components or deliberately application-owned source according to each implementation’s supported modes. Source adoption means maintaining your adaptations; Go changes require a rebuild.

## One catalog, one icon family

[Explore the public ecosystem catalog](https://github.com/gorazana/.github/blob/main/catalog/README.md) and [shared icons](https://github.com/gorazana/.github/blob/main/catalog/assets/gallery.svg). Both are generated from [Gorazana/ecosystem](https://github.com/gorazana/ecosystem), the canonical research repository (access may be required). No duplicated editable package list or roadmap is maintained here.

**66 classified entries:** 28 reusable packages, 35 business modules, 2 development tools and **Gorazine**. Research entries are not released products.

**Gorazine** is the owner-named, Filament-inspired administration composition—not a replacement identity, tenant or business engine. **Gorazana Create** is the proposed premium-quality terminal application assembler: simple/API/full-stack profiles; Go HTML or verified Inertia Vue/React; Gorazana or custom providers; selected business modules.

Business domains include CMS, Accounts/UMS, Records/DMS, Directory, Commerce, CRM, support, projects, workforce, finance, governance and education. The Modules package provides registration infrastructure; it is not the domain catalog itself.

## Engineering principles

Native services first; explicit host identity and tenant admission; public contracts; immutable applied migrations; safe publishing; no fake providers; evidence over claims. Static checks, real databases/browsers, live providers, physical devices and independent review are separate. All releases stay **0.x.x** while real applications build field evidence.

Gorazana is independent of and not endorsed by the Goravel maintainers.
